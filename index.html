The Itinerary

17 Days, Zero Boring Moments

Mountains, temples, beaches - every day is different.

✈️ Travel Phase
Day 01 - Dec 16

Fly to Kathmandu

Morning flight from Chennai. Arrive around 2 PM. Night bus to Pokhara. The adventure begins with the Himalayan skyline greeting you from the plane window.

✈️ Flight🛕 Kathmandu🚌 Night Bus
🥾 Trek Phase · 6 Days
Day 02 - Dec 17

Trek Begins: Ghorepani

Drive to Nayapul, then 21km of steep stairs through Tikhedhunga. Push to Ghorepani at 2,860m for an incredible sunset over the Annapurna range.

🥾 21km⬆️ +1,700m🌄 Sunset
Day 03 - Dec 18

Poon Hill Sunrise to Bamboo

Wake at 4:30 AM for the legendary Poon Hill sunrise at 3,210m. Annapurna and Dhaulagiri lit gold. Then descend through rhododendron forests to Bamboo camp.

☀️ 3,210m🌺 Rhododendrons24km
Day 04 - Dec 19

Bamboo to Deurali

Steady climb along the Modi River. Rocky terrain with snow appearing above 3,000m. Tea house at 3,230m - the air gets thin and the views get massive.

🏕️ 3,230m❄️ Snow9km
Day 05 - Dec 20

Summit: ABC at 4,130m

THE BIG DAY! 360 degree panorama of Annapurna I, Machapuchare, and surrounding giants. Unforgettable sunrise at the base camp.

🏔️ 4,130m📸 360 Views🌅 Sunrise
Day 06 - Dec 21

Descent to Jhinu Hot Springs

25km descent dropping 2,500m. Your knees will feel it but the reward is natural hot springs at Jhinu Danda. Soak those tired muscles.

⬇️ -2,500m♨️ Hot Springs25km
Day 07 - Dec 22

Trek Complete - Pokhara

Final 15km to Nayapul. Jeep back to Pokhara. Celebrate at Lakeside with momos, beer, and stories. You conquered the Himalayas!

🏁 Finish🍻 Celebrate🏞️ Lakeside
🛕 City Phase · Kathmandu
Day 08-11 - Dec 23-26

Kathmandu Culture

Bus back to KTM. Explore Pashupatinath, Boudhanath stupa, Thamel cafes. Shop for souvenirs, eat local food, and soak in the culture.

🛕 Temples🛍️ Thamel🍜 Local Food
🚆 Journey South
Day 12 - Dec 27

Cross Border to Gorakhpur

Cross the Nepal-India border. Reach Gorakhpur and board the train toward Goa. The landscape shifts from mountains to plains.

🚆 Train🛂 Border🇮🇳 India
🏖️ Beach Phase · Goa
Day 13-15 - Dec 28-30

Goa Beach and Chill

From frozen peaks to warm sand. Beaches, seafood, sunsets, and pure vibes. The perfect contrast after a week in the mountains.

🏖️ Beaches🍤 Seafood🌅 Sunsets
Day 16-17 - Dec 31-Jan 1

Goa to Chennai

Final day in Goa, then train home to Chennai. 17 days, 3 destinations, 1 unforgettable adventure. Trip complete!

🚂 Train🏠 Home🎉 Complete

Good to Know

Travel Tips

💵

Carry NPR Cash

ATMs limited on trek. Carry enough Nepali Rupees before starting.

🎒

Pack Light

Every kilo counts at altitude. Rent gear in Pokhara if needed.

🍜

Dal Bhat Power

Best value meal on trek. Unlimited refills. Higher altitude = higher prices.

🚂

Book Trains Early

Gorakhpur to Goa and Goa to Chennai fill fast. Book weeks ahead.

🧊

Microspikes

Essential above 3,500m. Icy paths near ABC can be dangerous without them.

💊

Diamox for AMS

Start 1 day before high altitude. Drink 3-4L water daily above 3,000m.
